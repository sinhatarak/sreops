# About

I'm **Tarak Nath Sinha**, a Site Reliability Engineer and **CNCF Kubestronaut**. I help teams run Kubernetes in production without the 3 a.m. surprises.

SREOPS is where I write down what I learn from keeping real systems healthy: how clusters behave under pressure, why alerts fire, and how to recover quickly when something breaks. Everything here comes from hands-on work, not slideware.

## What I do

- **Kubernetes operations:** building, upgrading and securing clusters that stay boring in the best way.
- **Reliability engineering:** defining SLOs, and building monitoring and alerting that wakes people only when it matters.
- **Incident response and recovery:** runbooks, backups and restore drills that have been tested before the outage, not during it.
- **Linux and automation:** repeatable, version-controlled infrastructure instead of one-off fixes.

## Why Kubestronaut

Kubestronaut is CNCF's recognition for people who have passed every Kubernetes certification it offers. Three of those exams are hands-on, performance-based tests in a live terminal, so the title reflects practical skill, not memorised answers. [See the full credential breakdown](kubestronaut.md).

## How I work

1. **Measure before changing.** Decisions come from metrics and logs, not guesses.
2. **Automate the second time.** The first fix is manual; the repeat becomes code.
3. **Write it down.** A runbook that exists beats expertise that lives in one person's head.
4. **Design for failure.** Everything breaks eventually, so I plan how it breaks.
