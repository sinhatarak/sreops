## Why Kubestronaut

Kubestronaut is CNCF's recognition for people who have passed every Kubernetes certification it offers. Three of those exams are hands-on, performance-based tests in a live terminal, so the title reflects practical skill, not memorised answers. [See the full credential breakdown](kubestronaut.md).

## How I work

1. **Measure before changing.** Decisions come from metrics and logs, not guesses.
2. **Automate the second time.** The first fix is manual; the repeat becomes code.
3. **Write it down.** A runbook that exists beats expertise that lives in one person's head.
4. **Design for failure.** Everything breaks eventually, so I plan how it breaks.
